# The nine verticals: buyers, words, proof and cautions

Use this to fit positioning, pillars and examples to the sector the person serves. It carries no statistic, market size, benchmark or fact about a named company. Any figure used in an asset comes from the person or from a page fetched in the session, with its link.

| Vertical | Typical buyers and readers | Words the buyers use | What proof looks like | Cautions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Logistics tech | heads of logistics, supply chain, transport and operations at shippers, carriers, retailers and distributors | dispatch, route planning, first mile, last mile, delivery promise, visibility, proof of delivery, fleet use | operational before and after measures the customer tracked, deployment scope the customer allows to be named | a software vendor is not a carrier; do not frame a business product like a consumer delivery app |
| Fintech | CFOs, finance controllers, treasury, payments, risk and compliance teams | reconciliation, spend control, collections, audit trail, compliance, controls | process time and error measures the customer reported, audit or regulator acceptance where public | regulated; no advice, no promised returns; rule facts from the regulator's own page |
| SaaS | depends on the product: functional heads, IT, operations | adoption, onboarding, integrations, renewal, expansion, admin effort | customer stories shared with permission, usage outcomes the customer measured | do not assume a self serve motion for a product sold through sales teams |
| Vertical SaaS | operators of the industry served, for example sales and distribution heads at consumer goods firms | the industry's own words for its workflows (outlets, distributors, field teams, orders) | workflow outcomes in that industry's terms | use the industry's vocabulary, not generic software words |
| AI native companies | functional heads with IT, security and data teams | agents, evaluation, accuracy as measured, human review, governance, data handling | deployments with results the customer agrees to share, published evaluations | no capability claim without the vendor's own page or a published evaluation; no hype words |
| ITeS (IT and IT-enabled services) | CIOs, CTOs, engineering heads, global capability centre leaders, procurement | delivery, engagement model, service levels, managed services, outcomes, talent, modernisation | client outcomes shared with permission, delivery track record, certifications | services are sold through relationships and proposals; no "free trial", "activation" or "aha moment" |
| Telecom | enterprise IT and network heads buying services; operations heads at operators | connectivity, coverage, network, service levels, managed services, enterprise solutions | service performance the customer reported, public filings | a network is not sold like a software app; say which telecom business the person is in |
| Software companies | engineering, IT and product leaders | deployment, licensing, upgrades, support, developer experience, security review | reference customers, technical evaluations, public release notes | separate what is shipped from what is on a roadmap |
| Cybersecurity | CISOs, security operations, risk, compliance and IT | detection, response, exposure, incidents, compliance frameworks, controls | audits, certifications, customer references, published research with its method | never speculate about a breach or name a victim without a public source; no fear selling; no guarantees |

Company stages these notes were written for: pre Series A through Series C. Regions: India, Middle East and Africa, Southeast Asia, United Kingdom, United States, Western Europe. These notes cover only the nine sectors above; for any other sector, work only from what the user typed and pages fetched in the session.
