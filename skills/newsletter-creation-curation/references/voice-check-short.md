# Short voice check (carried inside this skill)

Run on the issue and every derived asset before they are shown. For a full audit with line by line flags, the handcrafted-voice-audit skill in this plugin can be used, but this check stands on its own.

1. No long or short dashes, and no spaced hyphen used as a dash. Hyphens inside words are fine.
2. None of these words or phrases: game-changer, game-changing, leverage, dive in, dive deep, delve, tapestry, elevate, elevated, unlock, seamless, seamlessly, landed hard, resonated, struck a chord, genuine unlock, genuinely, honestly, straightforward, revolutionize, supercharge, harness, empower, in today's world, ever-evolving, next level, it's important to note, testament to, the rise of, keep coming back to, sat with me, rubric, move the needle, here's the thing, groundbreaking, revolutionary, transformative, comprehensive, robust, holistic, synergy, paradigm shift. "Landscape", "navigate", "dynamic" and "just" only in their literal sense.
3. "Thought leader" is never applied to the writer by themselves or for them. Text inside quotation marks from a source stays as the source wrote it.
4. No stock openers such as "Humbled to share", "Thrilled to announce" or "Excited to share".
5. Prose under each heading; no bullet lists in the body unless the user's own template uses them. Headers are fine.
6. No "That's not X. That's Y" contrast lines or their variants ("They are not just X. They are Y.", "We don't just X; we Y"), no "First... Second... Third..." in prose, no staccato fragments of one to three words, no neat punchlines, no hyperbole.
7. No exclamation marks, emoji, Unicode bold letters, arrows or checkmarks.
8. The writer's own raw phrases and takes are kept where they carry meaning. Indian English is fine.
9. Reading level fits the audience: secondary school to graduate for most professional readers.
10. Counts, numbers, links and dates are checked with scripts/asset_check.py, never estimated.
