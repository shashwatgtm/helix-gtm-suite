# Short voice check (carried inside this skill)

Run on every asset before it is shown. For a full audit with line by line flags, the handcrafted-voice-audit skill in this plugin can be used, but this check stands on its own.

1. No long or short dashes, and no spaced hyphen used as a dash. Hyphens inside words are fine.
2. None of these words or phrases: high-impact shift, game-changing, use, dive in, dive deep, examine, mix, elevate, elevated, open, smooth, smoothly, landed hard, resonated, struck a chord, genuine open, genuinely, honestly, straightforward, revolutionize, supercharge, harness, empower, in today's world, ever-evolving, next level, it's important to note, proof to, the rise of, keep coming back to, sat with me, scoring methodology, move the needle, here's the thing, groundbreaking, revolutionary, transformative, comprehensive, robust, complete, synergy, paradigm shift. "Landscape", "navigate", "dynamic" and "just" only in their literal sense.
3. "Thought leader" is never applied to the person by themselves or for them.
4. No stock openers such as "Humbled to share", "Thrilled to announce" or "Excited to share".
5. Credentials and chest thumping never lead. The first line is about the work, the reader or a lived moment.
6. No "That's not X. That's Y" contrast lines or their variants ("They are not just X. They are Y.", "We don't just X; we Y"), no "First... Second... Third..." in prose, no staccato fragments of one to three words, no neat punchlines, no hyperbole.
7. No exclamation marks, emoji, Unicode bold letters, arrows or checkmarks.
8. The person's own raw phrases are kept where they carry meaning. Indian English is fine.
9. Reading level fits the audience: secondary school to graduate for most professional readers.
10. Counts and dates are checked with scripts/count_fields.py, never estimated.
