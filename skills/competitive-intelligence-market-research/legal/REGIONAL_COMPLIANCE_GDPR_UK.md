# Regional Compliance Review (GDPR / UK GDPR)

_Last updated: 2026-03-08_

## Scope
Applies when serving EU/EEA or UK users.

## Required Controls Checklist
- Privacy notice published and accessible
- Lawful basis recorded for personal data processing
- Data subject request workflow in place
- Sub-processor list maintained and available on request
- International transfer safeguards documented
- Data retention schedule documented
- Incident response process includes regulatory notification decisioning
