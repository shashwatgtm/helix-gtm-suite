# Privacy Policy (GTM Helix CI App)

_Last updated: 2026-03-08_

## 1. Who We Are
GTM Helix operates competitive intelligence app workflows delivered via ChatGPT and `gtmhelix.com`.

## 2. Data We Collect
- Account identifiers (email, auth IDs via Clerk)
- Usage metadata (scenario selected, run timestamps, completion events)
- User-provided business inputs (competitor names, objectives, constraints)
- Billing metadata from Dodo Payments (plan, invoice IDs, payment status)

## 3. How We Use Data
- Provide and improve app outputs
- Enforce free-to-paid limits and account entitlements
- Customer support and incident resolution
- Security monitoring and abuse prevention
- Legal and compliance obligations

## 4. Data Processors
- Clerk (authentication)
- Firebase (application data and analytics storage)
- Railway (app hosting/runtime where applicable)
- Dodo Payments (merchant-of-record/payment processing)

## 5. Retention
- Operational logs: 90 days default
- User workspace records: until deletion request or contract end
- Billing records: as required by Dodo/legal obligations

## 6. User Rights
Users may request access, correction, deletion, or export of personal data by emailing `shashwat@gtmhelix.com`.

## 7. Cross-Border Data Transfers
Where applicable, data may be processed outside a user’s home jurisdiction with appropriate safeguards and vendor contractual protections.

## 8. Security
We implement access controls, key management, auditing, and incident response procedures documented in repository operations policies.

## 9. Contact
Privacy/contact requests: `shashwat@gtmhelix.com`.
