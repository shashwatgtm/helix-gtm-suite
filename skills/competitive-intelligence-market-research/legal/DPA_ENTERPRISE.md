# Data Processing Addendum (Enterprise Buyers)

_Last updated: 2026-03-08_

## 1. Roles
- Customer: Data Controller
- GTM Helix: Data Processor

## 2. Processing Purpose
To deliver competitive intelligence workflows, account operations, and support services.

## 3. Categories of Data
- User account identifiers
- User-entered business information
- Usage and audit logs

## 4. Sub-processors
- Clerk, Firebase, Railway, Dodo Payments (as applicable)

## 5. Security Measures
- Role-based access controls
- Secrets management and key rotation through platform controls
- Encryption in transit and at rest per sub-processor capabilities
- Audit logging for admin actions

## 6. Data Subject Requests
GTM Helix assists customers with access/deletion/correction requests via `shashwat@gtmhelix.com`.

## 7. Breach Notification
GTM Helix notifies enterprise customer contacts without undue delay after confirming a material incident affecting customer data.

## 8. Deletion/Return
Upon contract termination, customer data is deleted or returned per agreed retention terms.
