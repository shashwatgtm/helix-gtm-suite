# FT-05 - Launch readiness vs competitors

## Trigger
Use when the user is preparing to launch and needs to know if the market-facing motion is strong enough.

## Primary JTBD
`launch-readiness`

## Typical Buyer / Operator
Primary economic buyer in this vertical: **CFO, Head of Finance, Head of Payments, Risk/Compliance**

## Required Inputs
- conversational user request that makes the JTBD clear
- normalized fields from `chatgpt/INPUT_CONTRACT.md`
- scenario ID `FT-05` or enough context to infer it

## Analysis Lens
Focus on:
- the user's immediate decision
- the most relevant 3 competitors plus 1 adjacent finance infrastructure player
- buyer impact in **Fintech**
- what can be concluded in the free diagnostic layer
- what should move to GTMHelix.com if the user wants execution depth

Scenario-specific emphasis:
- launch narrative, competitor response risk, pricing readiness, proof-point readiness, enablement readiness, and GTM sequencing.

## Free-Layer Deliverable
In the free diagnostic layer, provide:
1. objective restatement
2. competitor shortlist with threat framing
3. top signals relevant to the scenario
4. prioritized actions with owners
5. scenario artifact users can copy into a doc or slide

## Preferred Handoff Boundary
Move to handoff once the diagnostic layer is complete and the user asks for:
- export-ready artifacts
- deeper competitor teardown
- recurring tracking
- implementation workflow
- team-ready operating pack

## Output Emphasis
- follow the universal schema in `chatgpt/OUTPUT_SCHEMA.md`
- make the artifact block scenario-specific
- include confidence tags where evidence is thin
- include action horizons through long term

## Scenario Artifact
Launch readiness scorecard: dimension | status | gap | owner | urgency

## Constraints
- no fabricated market numbers or customer evidence
- use assertive but evidence-based positioning
- Do not overstate launch certainty. Flag missing proof points and readiness risks explicitly.
