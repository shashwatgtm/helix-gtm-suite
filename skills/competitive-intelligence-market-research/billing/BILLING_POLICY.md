# Billing, Refund, and Tax Policy

## Billing Terms
- 100% advance payment required before execution.
- Pre-approval MVP: paywall disabled.
- Post-approval: free tier limited by product policy (30% activity depth and 2 scenario runs).

## Refund and Cancellation
- No refunds.
- No cancellations.

## Tax Handling
- Dodo Payments is Merchant of Record and handles tax computation/collection/remittance per jurisdiction.

## Revenue Recognition (Current Stage)
- Detailed internal revenue recognition/reporting workflow deferred until cumulative app revenue reaches USD $5,000.
- Until then, rely on Dodo reporting exports for operational accounting visibility.
