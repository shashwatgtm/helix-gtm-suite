✅ FOLLOWER GROWTH RATE
Formula: (New followers this month / Total followers) × 100

Benchmarks:
- 5-10% monthly: Good
- 10-20% monthly: Great
- 20%+ monthly: Excellent
- <5%: Growth stalled

✅ PROFILE VIEWS
Metric: People clicking to your profile after seeing content

What it tells you:
- High profile views but low follower conversion = profile not optimized
- High profile views + high follower conversion = working well

Fix if needed:
- Optimize headline (does it explain what you do?)
- Optimize about section (does it give them reason to follow?)
- Optimize featured content (does it showcase your best work?)

✅ INBOUND DMs
Metric: People reaching out (jobs, consulting, partnerships, customers)

This is THE metric for "is personal brand working?"

Benchmarks:
- 2-5 quality DMs/week: Good
- 5-10 DMs/week: Great
- 10+ DMs/week: Excellent
- 0-1 DMs/week: Brand not strong enough yet

Quality matters:
- Spam DMs don't count
- "Can I pick your brain?" (time waste) don't count
- Real opportunities count (jobs, customers, partnerships)

✅ WEBSITE TRAFFIC FROM SOCIAL
Track in Google Analytics: Source = LinkedIn, Twitter

What to track:
- Clicks from social to website
- Conversion rate (clicks → signups/demos)
- Which posts drive most traffic

Why it matters:
- Social media → website → leads → customers
- This is actual business impact
- Not just engagement for engagement's sake

✅ LEADS/CUSTOMERS FROM SOCIAL
Track: "How did you hear about us?" → Social media

This is ULTIMATE metric for B2B.

Setup:
- Ask in demo form: "How did you hear about us?"
  * Options: LinkedIn, Twitter, Google, Referral, Other
- Track in CRM: Source = Social
- Attribute revenue to social channel

Benchmarks (depends heavily on industry, stage):
- Series A: 20-40% of pipeline from founder's personal brand
- Series B: 10-20% of pipeline from social
- Series C+: 5-10% of pipeline from social (more channels)
