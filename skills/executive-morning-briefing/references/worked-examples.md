# Worked Examples — Morning Briefing

Full set of role-specific examples showing exact tone, structure, and prioritization.
Referenced from SKILL.md — loaded when generating briefings to match voice and format.

## Examples

### Sales IC — Prospect Responses

```
One thing this morning.

Your top mid-market prospect replied to your proposal last night—positive tone, they want to discuss pricing on a call this week. Worth responding before your 10am so you control the timing.
→ "Thanks for the quick reply. I'm free Thursday 2-4pm or Friday morning—what works?" Adjust this?

Your other deals are quiet. Pipeline review at 2pm—you're pacing ahead of quota, so no stress there.

That's your morning.
```

### Sales IC — Nothing Happening

```
Clear morning. No prospect responses overnight.

You have 3 follow-ups due today (in your task list). First meeting is the team standup at 9:30.

That's it.
```

### Sales Manager — Rep Needs Help

```
One fire this morning.

Jordan's largest deal — an enterprise account in procurement (~$300K) — is at risk. Procurement pushed back on pricing and their CFO is now involved. Jordan asked for help in #sales last night—this probably needs a call with you, not a Slack reply.

If one thing before standup: get context from Jordan and decide if you need to join the next call.

Rest of the team is tracking to quota. Two reps are light on activity this week—might be worth a pulse check in 1:1s.

Where do you want to start?
```

```
Safe to ignore: comp plan thread (not due until next week), SKO planning (delegated to ops).

What's your priority?
```

### Manual Mode (No Integrations)

When user provides data manually via paste or screenshot:

**User shares:** Calendar screenshot + pastes two Slack threads + says "MRR is $847K, down from $860K last month"

```
One thing to watch, otherwise light morning. (~60 sec read)

MRR dropped to $847K from $860K—about 1.5% down. Not alarming on its own, but worth knowing the cause. Any churn you're aware of, or timing of renewals?

From what you shared:

The strategic account renewal thread—they're asking for a discount. Priya's handling but flagged it for your awareness. No action needed unless you want to weigh in.

The product roadmap thread—engineering wants your input on Q2 priorities. Not urgent, but they're meeting Thursday. Worth a quick reply before then.

Your calendar shows a board prep at 2pm and 1:1 with Sarah at 4pm. Morning looks clear.

Anything else you want me to look at, or is that the full picture?
```

Note the phrasing: "From what you shared" signals partial data without apologizing. Ends with an invitation to add more context.

### Mixed Mode (Some Integrations Available)

Calendar connected, but Slack and email are not:

```
Two meetings need attention, calendar only. (~45 sec read)

Your 10am is a contract review with a strategic account—first meeting with their legal team. Might want 15 minutes to prep.

Your 3pm pipeline review has 8 attendees and no agenda. Could get long. Worth checking if there's a pre-read.

Calendar's clear otherwise until 5pm.

I don't have your Slack or email today—anything urgent in your channels you want me to know about?

What's your priority?
```

Note: One prompt for missing sources, then delivers value with what's available. No apologies, no excessive explanation.

### Afternoon Check-in (Non-Morning Use)

User asks "catch me up" at 3pm:

```
One thing since this morning.

The enterprise deal moved—they sent a revised proposal at 1pm. Numbers are close to what you discussed. Probably worth a look before your 4pm if you want to counter today.

Everything else is tracking. Your 4pm is the weekly sync, then you're clear.

Anything specific you want me to check?
```

Note: Framing shifts from "this morning" to "since this morning." Doesn't rehash items from the AM briefing.

### Weekend Quick Check

User asks "anything urgent?" on Saturday:

```
Nothing urgent since Friday.

One thing for Monday: the board deck placeholder is still open—Sarah pinged Friday afternoon asking if you'd get to it over the weekend. No pressure, just flagging.

Otherwise quiet. Enjoy your weekend.
```

Note: Lighter tone, explicit acknowledgment that it's the weekend, no expectation of action.
