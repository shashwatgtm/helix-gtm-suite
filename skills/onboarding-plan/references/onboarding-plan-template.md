# GUARD Onboarding Success Plan — Activate Mode Reference

## 4-Phase, 90-Day Framework

### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-14)
**Goal:** Establish the relationship, define success, complete technical setup.

**CSM actions:**
- Kickoff call: introduce CS team, set expectations, confirm goals
- Sales-to-CS handoff debrief: what was sold, what was promised, who are the stakeholders
- Define success criteria: 3-5 measurable outcomes the customer expects by Day 90
- Technical setup: provisioning, SSO, integrations, data migration kick-off
- Assign internal champion: identify who will drive adoption on the customer side

**Milestone:** Kickoff complete, success criteria documented, technical environment live.

**Risk triggers:**
- No kickoff scheduled by Day 5 → escalate
- Sales promised features that do not exist → flag immediately, manage expectations
- No clear internal champion identified → CSM must proactively identify one
- Technical setup blocked by customer IT → offer assisted setup, escalate if needed

### Phase 2: Activation (Days 15-30)
**Goal:** Achieve the first value milestone, enable the champion.

**CSM actions:**
- First training session: core workflow, not product tour
- Enable champion: give them the tools to train their own team
- Achieve first measurable value: the customer should see one concrete win
- Check: are users logging in? Is the champion engaged?
- Document the first win: this becomes ammunition for future QBRs and renewals

**Milestone:** First value milestone achieved, champion enabled, users logging in regularly.

**Risk triggers:**
- No logins by Day 20 → immediate outreach, offer hands-on session
- Champion not engaging → identify alternate champion
- Customer unclear on what "success" looks like → re-run success criteria workshop
- Training postponed multiple times → customer may be deprioritizing

### Phase 3: Adoption (Days 31-60)
**Goal:** Broaden usage beyond initial users, integrate into daily workflows.

**CSM actions:**
- Expand training to broader team (batch sessions, self-serve resources)
- Workflow integration: help embed the product into existing processes
- Second value milestone: deeper or broader than the first
- Mid-point check-in with exec sponsor: brief on progress, flag any concerns
- Begin documenting value for the first QBR

**Milestone:** Broader team adoption, product integrated into daily workflow, second value milestone.

**Risk triggers:**
- Usage plateauing at initial users only → diagnose blocker (training, permissions, relevance)
- Exec sponsor unresponsive → escalate through champion
- Customer feedback turning negative → address immediately, do not wait for QBR

### Phase 4: Optimization (Days 61-90)
**Goal:** Establish the health baseline, seed expansion, prepare for first business review.

**CSM actions:**
- Run first Gauge mode health assessment (establishes the baseline score)
- Identify advanced features or use cases for future expansion (seed, do not push)
- Prepare the first QBR/business review (use Review mode)
- Transition from onboarding cadence to ongoing CS cadence
- Document the full value story: before → after with data

**Milestone:** Health baseline established, first QBR completed, transition to steady-state.

**Risk triggers:**
- Health score below 60 at Day 90 → onboarding is not complete, extend with intervention plan
- Customer cannot articulate value at Day 90 → value communication failure, fix before anything else
- No expansion signals by Day 90 → normal, but note for future monitoring

## Handoff Checklist: Sales → CS

Before the kickoff call, CSM should have answers to:

| Item | Source | If Missing |
|------|--------|------------|
| What was sold (products, seats, tier) | Contract / CRM | Ask Sales |
| Why they bought (pain point, use case) | Sales notes | Ask customer at kickoff |
| Who made the decision (economic buyer) | Sales notes | Ask Sales |
| Who will use it (end users) | Sales notes | Ask customer at kickoff |
| What was promised (features, timelines, SLAs) | Sales notes / proposal | Critical — must verify |
| Competitive context (who else they evaluated) | Sales notes | Nice to have |
| Success criteria (what does "working" look like) | Sales notes | Define at kickoff |
| Timeline pressure (when do they need it live) | Sales notes | Ask customer at kickoff |

**Red flag:** If Sales cannot answer 5+ of these items, the handoff is incomplete. Do not proceed without filling the gaps.

## Segment Adaptation

### Enterprise (200+ employees, $100K+ ARR)
- Full 4-phase with dedicated CSM
- Weekly touchpoints in Phases 1-2, bi-weekly in 3-4
- Executive sponsor engagement at kickoff and Day 60
- Formal success plan document shared with customer

### Mid-Market (50-200 employees, $20K-100K ARR)
- 4-phase with pooled or dedicated CSM
- Bi-weekly touchpoints
- Champion-led with CSM oversight
- Lightweight success plan (shared doc or email confirmation)

### SMB (under 50 employees, under $20K ARR)
- Compressed to 60 days (2 phases: Activate + Optimize)
- Digital-first with on-demand CSM support
- Self-serve training with check-in at Day 30
- Success plan = 3 bullet email with goals and milestones
